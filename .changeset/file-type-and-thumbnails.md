Helpers for a connector that takes files: `sniffFileType`, `extensionsFor` and `typeAllowed` read and check a file's type from its bytes, `describeImage` measures a picture and makes the thumbnail the approval surfaces draw (with the optional `IMAGES` binding on `GatewayEnv`), `upstreamFetchBytes` reads a file from the CMS as the editor, and `readBoundedBody` and `sha256Hex` are exported.
